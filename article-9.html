Creating Sweet Memories: Family Moments with RJ's

Published on Feb 4, 2026 • 5 min read

Table of Contents

    Some of our fondest memories are made around the simplest pleasures—and shared treats have a special way of bringing families together. At RJ's, we're honored to be part of your family traditions.

    "Every time I share RJ's with my grandchildren, I'm passing down the same joy my parents shared with me." - Long-time Customer

    Generations of Sweet Moments

    Since 1994, RJ's has been a part of New Zealand family life. Parents who grew up enjoying our licorice are now sharing it with their own children, creating a beautiful chain of sweet memories that spans generations.

    Family Traditions

    We love hearing stories about how RJ's has become part of family traditions. Movie nights, road trips, picnics in the park—our treats have been there for millions of special moments. There's something magical about sharing a bag of RJ's with the people you love.

    Creating New Memories

    Whether it's a quiet afternoon at home or a special celebration, RJ's is there to make moments sweeter. Our variety of flavors means there's something for everyone in the family—from the traditionalists who love classic black to the adventurers who go for our chocolate-covered options.

    Family Favorites

    Based on customer feedback, here are the top family favorites:

    • Mixed Bullets - Something for everyone
    • Raspberry Twists - A hit with the kids
    • Classic Black - The parents' choice
    • Chocolate Logs - Perfect for sharing

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Are RJ's products safe for children?
    Yes! Our products are made with quality ingredients and are suitable for all ages. However, like all candies, they should be enjoyed as part of a balanced diet.
    What's the best way to share with family?
    Our Mixed Bullets bags are perfect for sharing—they have a variety that appeals to different tastes!

    Conclusion

    At the heart of RJ's is a simple belief: life is better when it's shared. We're grateful to be part of your family moments, your celebrations, and your everyday joys. Here's to creating many more sweet memories together!

    Sarah is the Lead Confectioner at RJ's. She loves experimenting with new flavors and writing about the sweet science of candy making.
