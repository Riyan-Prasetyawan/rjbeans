Why We Are Special

Discover what makes RJ's the confectionery choice of New Zealand and beyond.

Palm Oil Free

We care about the environment. No palm oil is used in any of our products, helping protect vital rainforest ecosystems.

New Zealand Made

Proudly crafted in Levin, New Zealand, using local expertise, passion, and generations of confectionery knowledge.

Family Recipe

Based on the original recipe by Roger and Regan Halliwell, passed down with care through our company.

Natural Ingredients

We use real licorice root extract and natural flavorings—no artificial shortcuts, just quality ingredients.

Award Winning

Multiple industry awards recognize our commitment to excellence and innovation in confectionery.

Loved Worldwide

Exported to over 25 countries, RJ's has become a symbol of New Zealand quality around the globe.
