The Art of Licorice Varieties

Published on Feb 2, 2026 • 6 min read

Table of Contents

    When it comes to licorice, variety truly is the spice of life. At RJ's, we've perfected the art of creating diverse licorice experiences that cater to every palate and preference.

    "Licorice isn't just a candy—it's a world of flavors waiting to be explored." - Master Confectioner

    Classic Black Licorice

    The traditional favorite that started it all. Our classic black licorice is made using authentic licorice root extract, giving it that distinctive, bold flavor that has been cherished for generations. The texture is perfectly balanced—soft enough to chew easily but firm enough to satisfy.

    Raspberry Red Twists

    For those who prefer a fruitier experience, our raspberry red twists offer a delightful burst of berry sweetness. Made with real raspberry flavoring, these vibrant treats are as beautiful as they are delicious.

    Mixed Bullets Collection

    Our signature mixed bullets bring together the best of both worlds—chocolate and licorice in perfect harmony. Each piece is carefully coated in premium chocolate, creating a multi-layered taste experience.

    Seasonal Limited Editions

    Throughout the year, we release special seasonal varieties that incorporate unique flavors and limited ingredients. From summer berry blends to winter spice collections, there's always something new to discover.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What's the most popular variety?
    Our Mixed Bullets remain the fan favorite, followed closely by Classic Black Licorice.
    Are there sugar-free options?
    Yes! We offer a sugar-free range that uses natural sweeteners while maintaining great taste.

    Conclusion

    Whether you're a purist who loves traditional black licorice or an adventurer seeking new flavor combinations, RJ's has a variety that will delight your taste buds. Explore our full range and discover your new favorite!
