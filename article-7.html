Natural Ingredients: Our Promise

Published on Feb 3, 2026 • 6 min read

Table of Contents

    In an age of artificial flavors and synthetic additives, we believe in doing things differently. At RJ's, natural ingredients aren't just a marketing tagline—they're a fundamental part of who we are.

    "The best flavors come from nature. Our job is simply to bring them together." - RJ's Quality Team

    Real Licorice Root Extract

    Unlike many confectionery brands that rely on artificial licorice flavoring, we source genuine licorice root extract for our products. This natural ingredient has been used for centuries and provides that distinctive, authentic taste that sets our licorice apart.

    No Artificial Colors

    The vibrant colors you see in our products come from natural sources. Our raspberry licorice gets its beautiful red hue from natural colorants, and our black licorice maintains its rich color through careful processing of quality ingredients.

    Sustainable Sourcing

    We work closely with our suppliers to ensure all ingredients are sourced responsibly. From the sugar we use to the cocoa in our chocolate-covered varieties, every ingredient meets our strict quality and sustainability standards.

    Our Ingredient Standards

    Every ingredient that goes into RJ's products must meet these criteria:

    • No palm oil - protecting rainforests
    • No artificial flavors - keeping it real
    • Responsibly sourced - ethical partnerships
    • Quality tested - ensuring consistency

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Are your products vegan?
    Many of our products are suitable for vegans. Check the packaging for specific dietary information.
    Where do you source your licorice root?
    We source our licorice root extract from trusted suppliers in regions known for premium quality licorice cultivation.

    Conclusion

    When you choose RJ's, you're choosing a product made with care, using ingredients you can trust. Our commitment to natural ingredients isn't just about taste—it's about providing you and your family with confectionery you can feel good about enjoying.
