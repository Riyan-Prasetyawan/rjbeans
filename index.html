About us

Levin-local, licorice-loving Roger Halliwell and his son Regan James (that's who we got our RJ's name from!) started making licorice with the belief they could make it better than anything they could buy.

Roger and Regan had big dreams for RJ's and by 1999, they were delighted to be market leaders. The kiwis had done it! The factory in Levin was cranking, with tonnes of licorice being made daily to satisfy demand.
