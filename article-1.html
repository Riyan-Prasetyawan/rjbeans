The Secret Behind Our Licorice

Published on Feb 1, 2026 • 5 min read

Table of Contents

At RJ's, we believe that great confectionery starts with great ingredients. But the real secret isn't just what we put in—it's how we make it.

"We don't just make candy; we craft memories that stick with you." - Roger Halliwell, Founder

Our journey began in Levin, New Zealand, with a simple family recipe. Roger and Regan Halliwell wanted to create licorice that wasn't just good, but exceptional. They spent years perfecting the texture, striking the perfect balance between soft and chewy.

It's All in the Process

Unlike mass-produced confectionery, we take our time. Our cooking process is slower, allowing the flavors to develop fully. We use a high-pressure extrusion method that gives our licorice its signature texture—firm on the outside, but meltingly soft on the inside.

Sourcing the Best Ingredients

We also source our licorice root extract from the finest growers. This ensures that deep, authentic flavor that our fans know and love. Combined with real chocolate for our bullets and balls, it's a match made in heaven.

The Family Tradition

So next time you open a bag of RJ's, know that you're tasting a piece of New Zealand magic, crafted with passion and patience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RJ's licorice gluten-free?
While some of our products contain wheat, we are working on a gluten-free range. Please check the back of the pack for specific allergen information.
Where is it made?
All our licorice is proudly made in Levin, New Zealand.

Conclusion

The secret isn't one single thing—it's a combination of passion, patience, and premium ingredients. We hope you enjoy eating our licorice as much as we enjoy making it.
